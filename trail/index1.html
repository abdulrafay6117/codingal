About Anwar Tariq & Co.

Anwar Tariq & Co. stands as a distinguished firm of Chartered Accountants in Pakistan, recognized for its unwavering commitment to delivering premier professional services. We are dedicated to empowering individuals and enterprises through precise financial guidance, ensuring regulatory compliance, and fostering sustainable growth in an evolving economic landscape.

Leveraging extensive experience and strategic foresight, our firm provides accurate, timely, and insightful advice. We partner with our clients to navigate complex financial challenges, achieve their strategic objectives, and build robust financial health, thereby solidifying their success in both local and international markets.

Delivering comprehensive audit value, our independent assurance services enhance confidence in financial reporting, identify critical risks, and fortify internal control frameworks to ensure full regulatory compliance.

Taxation Services

We empower businesses to achieve optimal tax efficiency while maintaining absolute compliance. Our services encompass strategic tax planning, meticulous preparation, and adherence to all pertinent tax laws for individuals and corporations.

Advisory & Consultancy

Guiding our clients toward informed decisions for their business's future, our strategic advisory and consulting services extend to business planning, financial restructuring, and navigating diverse financial challenges to drive sustained growth.

With a special emphasis on family governance and family-owned businesses, we assist organizations in establishing robust governance frameworks and developing insightful long-term strategies for sustainable success.
